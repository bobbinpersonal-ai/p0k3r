# Where you can call, and what it takes

Researched September 2026 from the statutes and state agency pages cited below.
I read the sources; I am not a lawyer. Anything marked **confirm** is worth a
phone call to the agency before you rely on it — they answer, and it is free.

Encoded in `src/lib/regions/states.ts` under each state's `calling` block, with
`coldCallPosture()` as the one-line answer.

## The short version

| State | Cold call today? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Wyoming** | ✅ Yes | Notice filing with the AG, no bond, no renewal. State no-call rules bite above 225 calls a year. |
| **Colorado** | ✅ Yes | Likely exempt from seller registration if you never close on the phone. No-call list is **$0** at 0–4 employees. |
| Kansas | ⚠️ With care | Uses the federal registry as its own list. No separate registration found — confirm. |
| Missouri | ⚠️ With care | $50 per area code per quarter for the list. The exemptions don't cleanly fit an unlicensed network. |
| **Indiana** | ❌ Not yet | Must register with the AG first, and Indiana's rules are stricter than federal. $5,000 per violation. |

**Start in Wyoming and Colorado. Leave Indiana alone.**

---

## Colorado — the important one

Colorado requires a "commercial telephone seller" to register with the Attorney
General ($200, no bond). But **C.R.S. 6-1-302(1)(h)** excludes from that
definition a person who:

- solicits **without intent to complete** the sale during the call, and
- **does not** complete it during the call, and
- completes it only at a **later face-to-face meeting** — not a meeting whose
  sole purpose is collecting payment or delivering goods.

That is your model, almost word for word: you call to book a free in-home
inspection, the contractor quotes and closes in person.

**This is the linchpin, so protect it.** The exemption dies the moment someone
quotes a firm price on the phone or takes a card number. Your script already
does this correctly — keep it that way.

Separately, register for the **Colorado No-Call List** through the PUC. The fee
is tiered by employee count and is **$0 for 0–4 employees**. Scrubbing it is
required regardless of the seller exemption.

## Wyoming — the easiest

File a notice of activity with the Attorney General designating an agent for
service of process. No annual renewal. No bond found. The state's do-not-call
provisions apply above **225 unsolicited sales calls per year**, so a small
operation starting out may sit under the threshold entirely.

Wyoming's calling window is **8am–8pm local**, tighter than the federal 8am–9pm.

## Kansas

Kansas designates the **federal National Do Not Call Registry as the Kansas
no-call list** — one list, no extra purchase. The statute requires consulting it
before calling and at least every 30 days after. No separate telemarketer
registration appears in K.S.A. 50-670/50-670a; **confirm with the AG**.

Unrelated to calling: a roofing partner in Kansas still needs Attorney General
roofing registration.

## Missouri

The Attorney General runs its own no-call list at **$50 per area code per
quarter** — a real running cost, so work one metro rather than the state.

RSMo 407.1098 exempts a call by a natural person responding to a referral, or
working from their primary residence, or by a person **licensed by Missouri** in
a trade who is setting an appointment for that trade. The licensed-trade limb
does not fit an unlicensed referral network. **Confirm** whether the others do
before dialling.

## Indiana — don't

Annual registration with the Attorney General is required **before doing
business**, $50, renewed before 1 August. No bond. Failing to register is itself
a deceptive act.

The bigger problem: Indiana's exemptions **do not include the ordinary
established-business-relationship** that federal rules allow. A past enquiry
does not buy you a call. Penalties run to $5,000 per violation.

Inbound leads who filled in your form and ticked consent are a different matter.
Cold calling is the part to avoid.

## What applies everywhere, regardless of state

- **Scrub the National Do Not Call Registry.** First five area codes are free.
- **8am–8pm in the homeowner's local time**, not yours. Three time zones across
  five states.
- **Say who you are and that it's a sales call in the first sentence.** Federal
  Telemarketing Sales Rule, not a style choice.
- **"Take me off your list" ends it immediately and permanently.**
- **Manual dial only.** No autodialer to a mobile.
- **You're calling from California**, which requires all-party consent to
  record. Don't record without asking.

## Before your first dial

- [ ] Wyoming: file the AG notice of activity
- [ ] Colorado: register for the No-Call list (free at your size)
- [ ] Both: buy National DNC access for the area codes you'll work (first 5 free)
- [ ] Confirm the Colorado face-to-face exemption with the AG's office
- [ ] Never quote a firm price or take payment on a call
