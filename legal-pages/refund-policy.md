# Refund & Return Policy

## Returns
You have **[30 days]** from the delivery date to request a return. To be eligible, items must be unused, in original packaging, and in the condition you received them.

To start a return, email [support@spadrahouse.com] with your order number and reason for return. We'll send instructions once approved.

## Non-returnable items
- Clearance / final sale items
- Custom or personalized products
- Items returned without prior authorization

## Damaged or incorrect items
If your item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within [48–72 hours] of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We'll cover a replacement or refund at no cost to you — no return shipping required for our error.

## Return shipping
Unless the item is defective or incorrect, return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility. We recommend a trackable shipping method, since we can't issue a refund for returns lost in transit.

## Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll notify you of approval. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within **[5–10 business days]**.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled for a full refund only before they ship. Once an order has shipped, our standard return policy applies.
