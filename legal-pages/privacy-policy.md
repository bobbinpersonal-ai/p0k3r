# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date]

This policy explains what information Spadra ("we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect
- **Contact & order info** you provide at checkout: name, email, shipping/billing address, phone number.
- **Payment info**: processed directly by our payment provider (Shopify Payments / [Stripe/PayPal]) — we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
- **Automatically collected data**: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and similar analytics data via cookies and Shopify's built-in analytics.

## How we use it
- To process and fulfill your orders, including sharing your shipping address with our fulfillment partners (e.g. CJ Dropshipping and their suppliers) solely to ship your items.
- To respond to customer service requests.
- To send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing emails — you can unsubscribe anytime.
- To improve the site and prevent fraud.

## Sharing
We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with:
- Fulfillment/shipping partners, to the extent needed to deliver your order.
- Payment processors, to complete transactions.
- Service providers (e.g. email/analytics platforms), under confidentiality obligations.

## Cookies
We use cookies for cart functionality, analytics, and (if enabled) marketing. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to opt out of certain data uses. Contact us at [support@spadrahouse.com] to make a request.

## Data retention
We retain order information as long as needed for legal, accounting, and business purposes.

## Children's privacy
This site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in applicable regions), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: [support@spadrahouse.com].
