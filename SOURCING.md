## What PokerMerchant validates
They're poker-only (no blackjack/roulette/craps/baccarat) but go deep on **three chip material tiers** — this is the structural insight worth copying for the Poker vertical:
1. **ABS plastic** — budget tier, casual/party buyers
2. **Clay composite w/ metal inlay** — mid tier, tournament-style, biggest volume
3. **Ceramic, full-color print** — premium tier, custom/branded

Plus a custom-branding tool (chips/plaques/cards/tables) — worth a v2 consideration, not a launch requirement.

## Copy/compliance reminder (carried from Phase 1)
Keep all copy strictly fair-use/home-game framed. Avoid "cheating device" language on anything security/serialized (e.g. craps dice serial numbers = fairness/anti-cheat *for the table*, frame as "casino-authentic" not "detect cheaters"). This is the concrete GMC dishonest-behavior risk, not the equipment category itself.
