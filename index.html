Questions, answered

How fast is a screen repair, really?

About 45 minutes for most models. Walk in, grab a coffee, walk out with a new screen.

Do you buy broken iPhones?

Yes — cracked screens, dead batteries, even phones that won't turn on. The instant quote tool on the sell page adjusts for condition.

What's covered by the 90-day warranty?

Any defect in the parts we installed or the work we did. It doesn't cover new accidental damage, but we'll always give you a fair price to fix that too.

Do I need an appointment?

No — walk-ins welcome. Booking ahead through the repairs page just guarantees no wait.

Will my data be safe?

Yes. We never browse your phone, and for any device you sell us we factory-reset it in front of you and remove it from your iCloud on the spot.
